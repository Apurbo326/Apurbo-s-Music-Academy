Instructor Biography

Apurbo Karmokar

Apurbo Karmokar, the founder and lead instructor of Apurbo's Music Academy, is a passionate guitarist, composer, and educator with over a decade of experience in guitar performance and music education. Known for his innovative teaching approach, he emphasizes both technical mastery and creative expression, guiding students to unlock their full musical potential.

Apurbo is the driving force behind Guitar Never Lies, a platform, build and run by K H SAZZAD AREFEEN, dedicated to nurturing aspiring guitarists through structured lessons, workshops, and performance opportunities. His curriculum spans from fundamental techniques to advanced improvisation, ensuring students develop a solid foundation and the confidence to perform professionally.

Beyond teaching, Apurbo has contributed to numerous music projects, collaborated with local artists, and shared his knowledge through online tutorials and masterclasses. His dedication to music education has inspired countless students to pursue their passion for guitar, blending traditional methods with modern techniques for a comprehensive learning experience.

At Apurbo's Music Academy, he fosters a supportive environment that celebrates creativity, discipline, and the joy of playing guitar, making music learning an engaging and transformative journey for every student.
